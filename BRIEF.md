# Yuna K-Beauty Channel Website — Pilot Brief

## Problem
Global K-beauty viewers need a trustworthy, fast way to understand what Yuna checks and continue to the official YouTube channel.

## Audience
English-speaking skincare and K-beauty viewers, especially US shoppers, arriving from social profiles, video descriptions, or search.

## Primary surface
**Decide / Learn.** The homepage explains one promise per section and moves the visitor toward the official YouTube channel.

## Success signal
A visitor can identify within one viewport:

1. who Yuna is;
2. what evidence-led K-beauty means;
3. where to watch the official channel.

## Version boundary
This is a static GitHub Pages pilot. It does not collect user data, sell products, process payments, provide personalized medical advice, or host an API backend.

## Required preservation
- TikTok verification TXT at the repository root
- `terms.html`
- `privacy.html`
- public explanation of the private creator API sandbox

## Content truth boundary
- Use the verified public channel name, handle, channel ID, description themes, and public Shorts URLs.
- Do not invent testimonials, audience demographics, performance metrics, product rankings, medical claims, or brand partnerships.
- Do not present retailer rank as independent product quality.
- Do not call a product BUY/WAIT/SKIP on the website without a source packet for that exact product.
