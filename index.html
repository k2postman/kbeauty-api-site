The evidence route

Three checks before a verdict.

One screen, one job: identify the exact item, show what the source supports, then explain who the decision fits.

01

Pin the exact product.

Brand, product line, option, market, and date come first. Similar packaging is not treated as the same formula or offer.

02

Read the source boundary.

An official listing can support its own price, ingredients, or availability. It cannot prove that a product is universally better.

03

Make the decision conditional.

BUY, WAIT, or SKIP only makes sense with a reason and a fit. Uncertainty stays visible instead of being edited away.
